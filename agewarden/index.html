AGEWARDEN

Age verification from seconds of speech

AGEWARDEN tells a website whether a visitor is over eighteen. The visitor speaks briefly, and the site gets a yes or a no. It keeps nothing: no ID, no image of the face, no voiceprint. The website never touches the audio.

The check runs on serverless infrastructure that scales with demand. It is live at agewarden.ai, with self-serve sign-up and metered billing.

Results

Every release is tested on the same recordings, none of which it learned from. On them, AGEWARDEN is right 96.1% of the time, and no minor gets through.

In production, 97.8% of adults pass. A repair made to the running model cut wrongful rejections by 39% without letting a single minor through.

Against fakes, two defenses: a detector that separates live speech from a replayed recording almost perfectly (AUC 0.99), and a screen for synthetic voices.
