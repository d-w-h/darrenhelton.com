Who this is for
Customs brokers, importers, compliance teams

~15,000 active licensed customs brokers handle 97% of US import entries. Behind them: importers paying millions in duties, and compliance officers carrying personal liability under the DOJ Trade Fraud Task Force.

Customs brokers

The morning routine is checking CBP, Federal Register, USTR, and three newsletters before the first client call. One missed notice means a wrong rate on hundreds of entries. Rate lookups take 15-45 minutes each across multiple government sites.
Briefing covers regulatory changes across official sources, filtered by HTS code. Classify returns the full tariff stack in seconds.

Importers

An importer paying $8M in annual duties absorbs a 25% tariff increase as a direct margin hit. Trade compliance is often a part-time responsibility of a logistics or finance lead, not a dedicated team with dedicated tools.
Classify calculates total landed cost across all active tariff programs. Screen scores risk by HTS chapter and trade corridor.

Compliance officers

The DOJ Trade Fraud Task Force is pursuing criminal charges against individual officers. The False Claims Act has a $53M settlement precedent. The "reasonable care" standard now carries personal liability.
Entity screening, reclassification risk scoring, and a documented audit trail.

API reference
One REST API, every trade signal

JSON responses. No SDK required. Pass your API key in the x-api-key header.

Base URL: https://api.covenantandcrucible.com/v1
GET /v1/alerts
Recent alerts scored by severity. Filter by HTS code, country, or date range.
curl -H "x-api-key: YOUR_KEY" https://api.covenantandcrucible.com/v1/alerts?severity=critical&limit=10
GET /v1/alerts/{id}
Full alert detail with analysis, affected codes, action required, and source link.
curl -H "x-api-key: YOUR_KEY" https://api.covenantandcrucible.com/v1/alerts/alert-federal_register-fr-2026-06234-20260327
GET /v1/hts/{code}/stack
Tariff stack calculator. Every active program layered: Section 301, 232, AD/CVD, IEEPA, Section 122, FTA preferences.
curl -H "x-api-key: YOUR_KEY" https://api.covenantandcrucible.com/v1/hts/8471.30/stack
GET /v1/hts/{code}/feed
Unified timeline for an HTS code. Every ruling, case, rate change, and enforcement action in one feed.
curl -H "x-api-key: YOUR_KEY" https://api.covenantandcrucible.com/v1/hts/8471.30/feed
GET /v1/hts/{code}/risk
Reclassification risk score (0-100) for the chapter.
curl -H "x-api-key: YOUR_KEY" https://api.covenantandcrucible.com/v1/hts/8471.30/risk
GET /v1/ieepa/refunds
IEEPA refund eligibility tracker. Protest deadlines, Supreme Court ruling status, and related CBP guidance.
curl -H "x-api-key: YOUR_KEY" https://api.covenantandcrucible.com/v1/ieepa/refunds
GET /v1/sources
Health status and last ingestion timestamp for all monitored government sources.
curl -H "x-api-key: YOUR_KEY" https://api.covenantandcrucible.com/v1/sources
GET /v1/changes
Raw regulatory document feed. Every ingested document with metadata, before scoring.
curl -H "x-api-key: YOUR_KEY" https://api.covenantandcrucible.com/v1/changes?source=federal_register&limit=10
GET /v1/cards/{type}/{code}
Card-formatted JSON for sharing. Types: tariff-stack, ieepa-refund, risk-score.
curl -H "x-api-key: YOUR_KEY" https://api.covenantandcrucible.com/v1/cards/tariff-stack/8471.30
